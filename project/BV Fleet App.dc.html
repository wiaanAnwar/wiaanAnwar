WHAT CHANGED
  • Date-first. A two-month calendar drives everything; the fleet only shows what is genuinely free for those dates, with a "next free" date on the rest.
  • Sign-in and approvals. Phone + OTP gate, and corporate/agency requests route through a named approver before they reach BV.
  • Payment. Invoice, Bankak, mobile money or cash on collection, with VAT and a 25% deposit where it applies.
  • Trips do things. Live status timeline, call and WhatsApp the driver, amend, cancel with the policy shown.
  • Arabic and RTL across the whole app — switch it in Tweaks or from Account.
  • Real states. Search skeletons, an offline banner, submit failure with a WhatsApp fallback. Set the Network tweak to Offline to see them.
  • Fixes. Sequential references, admin-set SDG rate, per-plate vehicle instances, 44px hit targets, higher-contrast greys.

Real vehicle photography · the BV-side dispatch app · a live map for tracking · Arabic copy review by a native speaker before release.
